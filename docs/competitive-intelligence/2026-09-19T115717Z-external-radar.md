# External Signals

## A. Publishing platform policy — Apple Books has its own AI-generated disclosure contract

**CONFIRMED — Apple Books Formatting and Content Guidelines; checked 2026-09-19; page does not expose a verifiable publication/update date.**

Source: https://help.apple.com/itc/applebooksstoreformatting/en.lproj/static.html

Current Apple Books guidance states that book content with a **material portion generated by an AI-based tool** must be labeled with the **`AI Generated by` artist role** and must declare the AI-generated creation method in the book description. It separately says AI-assisted use — such as idea generation, research or editing — does not require this disclosure. The metadata section also identifies a missing AI-generated role as an error condition.

### User job

Before distributing the same book to multiple storefronts, the author needs a defensible answer about what the **current manuscript and assets actually came from**, without reconstructing production history from memory for each store.

### Concrete manual breakpoint

Without a current-revision origin summary, the operator must search runlogs/version history/import history and then manually translate that history into each storefront’s different fields/wording. Repeating the reconstruction is the waste; the product does **not** need to automate store submission to remove it.

### Transferable design

Keep two layers separate:

1. **Observed origin evidence**: what the workstation can prove about the current chapter/asset revision.
2. **Platform mapping/guidance**: how KDP, Apple Books, or another distributor currently asks the publisher to disclose it.

If evidence is missing or an external tool changed the file, output `UNKNOWN / NEEDS_USER_REVIEW`; do not infer authorship from prose style or metadata absence.

### Do not copy / overreach

Do not create an Apple Books uploader, marketplace account integration, compliance database, legal opinion engine or universal AI-content classifier. The official rule is evidence that the publishing job exists, not implementation authority.

## B. Recheck — Amazon KDP still uses a different generated-vs-assisted rule

**CONFIRMED — Amazon KDP Content Guidelines; checked 2026-09-19.**

Source: https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390

KDP currently requires publishers to inform Amazon of AI-generated text, images or translations when publishing a new book or editing/republishing an existing one. It does not require disclosure of AI-assisted content. Importantly, content actually created by an AI tool remains AI-generated even after substantial human edits.

### Product implication

This reinforces the existing #10 acceptance rule: an AI-generated chapter later edited by a human cannot be silently downgraded to “assisted.” It also demonstrates why the core data should be origin facts/receipts rather than a storefront-specific status string.

### What is not established

No user incident, rejected submission, enforcement event, revenue impact or probability of policy violation was observed for this repository. Severity therefore remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, not P0/P1/P2.
